# Data and Playback Policy

## Catalog metadata
Use a provider only after checking its API terms, attribution requirements, caching rules, rate limits, and commercial/non-commercial restrictions. A free-to-user product does not automatically grant the developer rights to redistribute provider data or artwork.

## Artwork
Use provider-hosted artwork only when the provider's terms permit the intended display. Otherwise use original graphics, licensed assets, or neutral placeholders. Do not download and rehost MovieBox artwork.

## Playback
Allowed source classes:
- Content owned by the project operator.
- Content licensed for the intended territory and method of distribution.
- Public-domain content after checking jurisdiction and edition-specific rights.
- Official provider embeds or handoffs where the provider explicitly permits them.

Disallowed implementation paths:
- Circumventing DRM, paywalls, authentication, geo-controls, or access restrictions.
- Reusing private or undocumented endpoints to obtain unlicensed streams.
- Embedding or proxying pirated streams.
- Claiming a title is playable when no authorized source has been verified.

## User messaging
When metadata exists but playback does not, say that playback is currently unavailable and provide a legitimate provider link if one is known. Do not show fake player controls or misleading play buttons.
